# Pricing and Fees

_Sample document for a demo. All prices below are invented for the demo._

## Service call fee

Every visit starts with a service call fee of 89 dollars. This covers travel,
the first 30 minutes on site and a written diagnosis. The fee is waived if you
go ahead with a repair over 400 dollars on the same visit.

## Standard labor rates

| When | Rate per hour |
|---|---|
| Weekday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM | 110 dollars |
| Saturday 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM | 165 dollars |
| Outside those hours, and all day Sunday | 220 dollars |

Labor is billed in 30 minute increments after the first half hour, which is
included in the service call fee.

## Emergency callout

An emergency callout carries a 150 dollar emergency fee on top of the service
call fee and the out of hours labor rate. See the emergency callout policy for
what counts as an emergency.

## Common flat price jobs

| Job | Flat price |
|---|---|
| Unblock a single sink or basin | 139 dollars |
| Replace a standard toilet fill valve | 185 dollars |
| Replace an outdoor spigot | 210 dollars |
| Annual boiler service | 189 dollars |
| Power flush a residential system | 695 dollars |

Flat price jobs include parts and labor and do not carry the service call fee
when booked in advance as the only item of work.

## Payment and deposits

We take cards, bank transfer and cash. Jobs quoted above 1,500 dollars require a
30 percent deposit before we order parts. Invoices are due 14 days from the
invoice date. Late invoices carry 1.5 percent interest per month.
