# Service Area and Hours

_Sample document for a demo. Brightwater Plumbing and Heating is a fictional company._

## Regular business hours

We answer calls and book appointments Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
and Saturday 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. We are closed on Sunday except for emergency
callouts. The office is closed on New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving
Day and Christmas Day.

## Towns we cover at standard rates

We serve the following towns with no travel surcharge:

- Brightwater
- Maple Ridge
- Fernbank
- Little Harbor
- Oakfield

## Towns we cover with a travel surcharge

We travel to Stonebridge, Pine Hollow and Westmill. These three towns carry a
flat travel surcharge of 45 dollars added to the service call fee, because they
sit outside our 20 mile core radius.

## Towns we do not serve

We do not take work in Granite Falls or anywhere across the county line to the
north. If you are outside our area we will say so on the phone before booking
and we will not charge you anything.

## Same day appointments

Same day slots open at 7:00 AM each weekday and are first come, first served.
If you call before 10:00 AM on a weekday there is usually a same day slot
available. Saturday same day work is limited to two slots.
