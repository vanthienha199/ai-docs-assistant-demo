# Water Heater FAQ

_Sample document for a demo._

## How long should a water heater last

A standard tank water heater lasts 8 to 12 years in our area. Hard water
shortens that. A tankless unit lasts 15 to 20 years with annual descaling.

## Why is my hot water running out faster than it used to

Usually sediment in the bottom of the tank, which cuts the usable volume. A
flush helps if the tank is under about 8 years old. If the tank is older than
that, a flush can disturb corrosion and start a leak, so we inspect first.

## What size do I need

As a rough guide, a 40 gallon tank suits 1 to 2 people, 50 gallons suits 3 to 4,
and 75 gallons or a tankless unit suits 5 or more. Bathroom count matters more
than square footage.

## Tank or tankless

A tankless unit costs more to install, mainly because of gas line and venting
work, and pays back over roughly 8 to 12 years through lower standing losses.
A tankless unit is a poor fit if your gas supply cannot carry the load or if
your incoming water is very hard and you will not keep up with descaling.

## Is a leaking tank repairable

A leak from a fitting, the drain valve or the temperature and pressure relief
valve is usually repairable. A leak from the tank body itself is not repairable
and the unit has to be replaced. We can tell you which it is on the first visit.

## Installation price range

A like for like 50 gallon tank swap usually lands between 1,450 and 1,950
dollars including the unit, fittings, disposal of the old tank and the permit.
A first time tankless install usually lands between 3,800 and 6,200 dollars
depending on gas and venting work.
