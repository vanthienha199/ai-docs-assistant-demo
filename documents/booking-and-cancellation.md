# Booking and Cancellation Policy

_Sample document for a demo._

## Appointment windows

We book 2 hour arrival windows, not exact times, because job length varies. You
get a text message when the technician is 30 minutes away. If we are going to
miss the window we call you before the window closes.

## Rescheduling

You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the start of your
window. Inside 24 hours we ask for the 89 dollar service call fee if the
technician has already been dispatched.

## Cancellation

Cancelling more than 24 hours ahead costs nothing. Cancelling inside 24 hours
carries a 45 dollar late cancellation fee. If nobody is home when the technician
arrives we charge the full 89 dollar service call fee as a missed appointment.

## Access requirements

Someone 18 or older has to be on site for the whole visit. We need clear access
to the work area and to the main water shutoff. If we cannot reach the shutoff
we may have to stop work and rebook, which counts as a missed appointment.

## Quotes

Written quotes are valid for 30 days. A quote is based on what we could see on
the day. If we open a wall or a floor and find something different we stop,
explain what changed and give you a revised price before carrying on.
