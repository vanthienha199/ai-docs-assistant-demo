# Warranty and Guarantees

_Sample document for a demo._

## Labor warranty

All labor we perform is guaranteed for 12 months from the date of the invoice.
If the same fault returns within 12 months we come back and fix it with no
service call fee and no labor charge.

## Parts warranty

Parts we supply carry the manufacturer warranty, which is 2 years on most
fittings and valves. Water heaters we supply and install carry a 6 year tank
warranty and a 2 year parts warranty from the manufacturer. We handle the
warranty claim with the manufacturer on your behalf for the first 2 years.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover:

- Parts you supplied yourself
- Damage from freezing where the property was left unheated
- Work another company altered after we completed it
- Blockages caused by items that should not be flushed
- Normal wear on washers, seals and anode rods

## Boiler warranty condition

A boiler warranty stays valid only if the boiler is serviced every 12 months.
We send a reminder 30 days before the service is due. If a service is missed the
manufacturer may refuse the claim and we cannot override that.

## How to make a claim

Call the office with your invoice number. We aim to have a technician on site
within 2 business days for a warranty visit, and same day where there is no hot
water or no heat in winter.
