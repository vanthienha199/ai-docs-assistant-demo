# Emergency Callout Policy

_Sample document for a demo._

## What counts as an emergency

We treat these as emergencies and attend outside normal hours:

- An uncontrollable leak you cannot isolate at a shutoff
- A burst pipe
- Sewage backing up into the property
- No heat when the outside temperature is below 40 degrees Fahrenheit
- A suspected gas smell, after you have called the gas emergency line first

## What does not count as an emergency

A dripping tap, a slow drain, one cold radiator, a noisy boiler and a running
toilet are not emergencies. We book those into normal hours at normal rates.

## Response times

Our target is on site within 2 hours of the call inside the core service area,
and within 4 hours for Stonebridge, Pine Hollow and Westmill. These are targets
and not guarantees during a freeze event, when call volume can be ten times
normal.

## What an emergency visit costs

An emergency visit is billed as the 89 dollar service call fee, plus the 150
dollar emergency fee, plus the out of hours labor rate of 220 dollars per hour.
A Sunday emergency callout therefore starts at 459 dollars for the first hour.

## Before we arrive

Turn off the water at the main shutoff if you can reach it safely. Turn off the
power to any appliance sitting in water at the breaker, not at the appliance.
Move what you can out of the way of the water. Do not use chemical drain
cleaner before we arrive, because it makes the work unsafe for the technician.
