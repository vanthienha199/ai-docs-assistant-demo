# Booking and Scheduling

Blue Harbor Plumbing is a sample company created for this demo. All policies below are fictional.

## How to book

Customers can book by phone, through the website form, or by replying to a previous invoice email.
Phone bookings are answered between 7am and 6pm on weekdays and between 8am and 1pm on Saturdays.
Website bookings submitted outside those hours are confirmed by 9am on the next working day.

## Appointment windows

Appointments are offered in three windows each weekday.

- Morning window, 8am to 11am
- Midday window, 11am to 2pm
- Afternoon window, 2pm to 5pm

The technician sends a text message when leaving the previous job, usually 30 to 45 minutes before arrival.

## Lead times

Routine work is normally scheduled within 3 working days.
Blocked drains and leaking pipes are normally scheduled within 24 hours.
During winter the lead time for routine work can extend to 5 working days because heating and hot water calls take priority.

## Rescheduling and cancellation

Customers may reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 4 hours before the start of the appointment window.
Cancellations inside 4 hours are charged a 45 dollar late cancellation fee.
If nobody is at the property when the technician arrives and there is no safe access, the visit is treated as a late cancellation.

## Access requirements

An adult over 18 must be present for the whole visit.
The technician needs clear access to the work area, the water shut off valve, and the meter.
If a pet is in the work area, the customer is asked to secure it before arrival.

## Running late

If the technician is delayed beyond the end of the booked window, the office calls the customer with a new estimate.
If the delay exceeds 90 minutes past the window, the customer may rebook at no charge and the call out fee is waived on the next visit.
