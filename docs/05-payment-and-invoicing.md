# Payment and Invoicing

Blue Harbor Plumbing is a sample company created for this demo. All terms below are fictional.

## Accepted payment methods

Blue Harbor accepts Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer, and cash.
Card payments are taken on site through the technician terminal or online through the link on the invoice.
American Express and cryptocurrency are not accepted.

## When payment is due

Residential invoices are due on the day of the visit.
Account customers and property managers are invoiced on 14 day terms.
A deposit of 30 percent is required for jobs quoted above 1500 dollars, and the balance is due on completion.

## Late payment

Invoices unpaid after 14 days receive a reminder by email.
After 30 days a late fee of 2 percent per month applies to the outstanding balance.
Accounts unpaid after 60 days are referred to a collections agency and future bookings are placed on hold.

## Quotes

Written quotes are free and are valid for 30 days.
A quote becomes fixed once the customer approves it in writing, by email or by signing the technician tablet.
If hidden damage is found after work starts, the technician stops, explains the position, and issues a revised quote before continuing.

## Invoices and receipts

An itemised invoice is emailed within 1 working day of the visit.
Invoices show labour hours, parts, the call out fee, any surcharge, and tax.
Duplicate invoices and annual statements are available on request at no charge.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 working days.
Cash payments are refunded by bank transfer, so the customer provides account details in writing.
