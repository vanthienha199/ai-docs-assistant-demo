# Emergency call outs

Blue Harbor Plumbing is a sample company created for this demo. All policies below are fictional.

## What counts as an emergency

An emergency is a situation causing active flooding, a burst pipe, a total loss of water, sewage backing up into the property, or no hot water in a property with an infant under 12 months or a resident over 70.
A dripping tap, a slow drain, or a running toilet is not treated as an emergency.

## Availability

Emergency cover runs 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including public holidays.
The emergency line is answered by a person, not a recorded menu.

## Response times

The target response time inside the metropolitan area is 2 hours.
In outer suburbs the target is 4 hours.
During severe weather events the office gives each caller a realistic estimate at the time of the call, because demand can triple within an hour.

## Emergency pricing

The emergency call out fee is 240 dollars between 9pm and 7am, and 160 dollars at other times outside normal hours.
Emergency labour is billed at 220 dollars per hour with a minimum charge of 1 hour.
The emergency fee is not credited against the repair, unlike the standard call out fee.

## What to do before the technician arrives

Turn off the water at the main shut off valve, which is usually near the front boundary or under the kitchen sink.
Switch off the electricity at the consumer unit if water is near any electrical fitting.
Move furniture and valuables away from the water and place a bucket under an active leak.
Do not use chemical drain cleaner before the visit, because it makes the drain unsafe for the technician to open.

## Insurance work

Blue Harbor provides a written report and itemised invoice suitable for an insurance claim at no extra cost.
The company does not bill insurers directly. The customer pays and claims the amount back.
