# Services and pricing

Blue Harbor Plumbing is a sample company created for this demo. All prices below are fictional.

## Standard call out fee

The standard call out fee is 89 dollars for appointments between 8am and 5pm, Monday to Friday.
The fee covers travel, the first 30 minutes of diagnosis, and a written quote.
If the customer approves the repair during the same visit, the call out fee is credited against the final invoice.

## Hourly labour rates

Standard labour is 120 dollars per hour, billed in 15 minute increments after the first hour.
Evening work between 5pm and 9pm is billed at 165 dollars per hour.
Weekend and public holiday work is billed at 190 dollars per hour.

## Fixed price jobs

Some common jobs are quoted at a fixed price so the customer knows the total before work starts.

| Job | Fixed price | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replace a kitchen tap | 240 dollars | 1 hour |
| Clear a blocked sink or basin | 180 dollars | 1 hour |
| Replace a toilet cistern | 320 dollars | 2 hours |
| Install a dishwasher connection | 280 dollars | 2 hours |
| Replace a hot water thermostat | 265 dollars | 90 minutes |

Fixed prices include labour and standard parts. Non standard parts are quoted separately before fitting.

## What is not included

Blue Harbor Plumbing does not carry out gas appliance servicing, roofing work, tiling, or electrical work beyond disconnecting and reconnecting an existing appliance.
Jobs that require a licensed gas fitter are referred to a partner company at no charge to the customer.

## Service area

Technicians cover the Blue Harbor metropolitan area and suburbs within 40 kilometres of the city centre.
Addresses between 40 and 70 kilometres out are accepted with a 45 dollar travel surcharge.
Addresses beyond 70 kilometres are not serviced.
