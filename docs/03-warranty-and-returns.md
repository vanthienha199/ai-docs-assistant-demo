# Warranty, Guarantees and Returns

Blue Harbor Plumbing is a sample company created for this demo. All terms below are fictional.

## Workmanship guarantee

All labour carried out by a Blue Harbor technician is guaranteed for 12 months from the invoice date.
If the same fault returns within 12 months, the return visit is free, including the call out fee and labour.
The guarantee covers the specific repair described on the invoice. It does not cover new faults on other parts of the system.

## Parts warranty

Parts supplied and fitted by Blue Harbor carry the manufacturer warranty, which is 2 years for taps and mixers, 5 years for hot water cylinders, and 1 year for pumps and small components.
Parts supplied by the customer are fitted on request but carry no warranty from Blue Harbor, and the labour guarantee is reduced to 3 months on those jobs.

## What the guarantee does not cover

The guarantee does not apply where the fault was caused by misuse, freezing, building movement, a third party working on the same system after the visit, or a blockage caused by items that should not enter the drain, such as wipes, nappies, or cooking fat.

## Making a warranty claim

Customers report a warranty issue by phone or email with the original invoice number.
The office responds within 1 working day and books a return visit within 3 working days.
Warranty visits are scheduled in the same appointment windows as normal work.

## Returns on parts not fitted

Unfitted parts in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
Special order items are not returnable once ordered from the supplier.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the part being received back.

## Complaints

A customer who is unhappy with a repair should contact the office within 14 days.
The service manager reviews the job, calls the customer within 2 working days, and sets out the options, which may include a free return visit, a partial credit, or a full refund of labour.
